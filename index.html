Sell calculator

Sell price is what the board pays you. Buy price is what each one cost you, and crops start at seed box ÷ 50. Everything marked “Est.” is an estimate, because board prices keep moving, about every 5 minutes. A rainbow price means the crop is at its max. The wiki average and max buttons only fill the crops that have wiki numbers.

Bonuses only touch crop sales and add together, so both is +15%.

Each jug takes 25 cocoa plus milk, and a full run makes up to 60 jugs. Prices come from the sell tab. Setup is about 260,000 (factory 50,000, cocoa trees 70,000, Oxbo3220 about 140,000), before barns and cows.
